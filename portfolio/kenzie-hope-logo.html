Kenzie & Hope

ProjectBoutique Logo Design
DisciplineBrand Identity
Completed2025

A feminine mark with a modern edge.

Designed for Kenzie & Hope, a boutique clothing store, this identity balances a clean fashion-forward wordmark with a distinctive ampersand at its center. Restrained typography and a muted rose palette give the mark a polished, feminine character while keeping it versatile across signage, packaging, social media, and retail materials.
