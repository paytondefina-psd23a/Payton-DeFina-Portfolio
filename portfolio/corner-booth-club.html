The Corner Booth Club

ProjectRestaurant Discovery Brand
DisciplineIdentity & Art Direction
Completed2026

Good spots. Local roots.

The Corner Booth Club is a restaurant discovery and review account built around seeking out memorable, distinctive places to eat. The identity draws from vintage supper clubs, worn membership cards, and classic restaurant ephemera to create a brand that feels established, social, and rooted in local character. A flexible family of marks and campaign graphics carries the system across profile images, social posts, and platform covers.
