Editorial beauty, reframed.

Created as a print editorial concept for Hourglass, Beauty Trends translates the brand’s polished, modern luxury into a fashion-forward magazine experience. Bold beauty imagery is paired with refined typography, product storytelling, and trend-led layouts to explore color, texture, light, and statement makeup.
